Original link: https://www.onezero.com/ecosystem/

Buy-side and sell-side firms come together to consume and distribute multi-asset class liquidity — plus access partner services for data analysis, post-trade and regulatory reporting without special integration.

Original link: https://www.onezero.com/data-source/

A next-generation cloud-based business intelligence toolkit that stores your quote and trade data and enables its transformation into advanced data analytics.

Original link: https://www.onezero.com/engagement/

Real-time analytics, trading insights, and outreach tools built to attract, activate, and retain traders. oneZero Engagement delivers dynamic trader content, turnkey integrations, and actionable insights, helping brokers drive platform usage, loyalty, and performance.

# 6. FX Mental Model

## Original Purpose

FX exists because real businesses need to exchange currencies.

Example:

```text
US Company
↓
Needs JPY
↓
Japanese Supplier
```

## Historical Evolution

```text
Physical Trade
↓
Price Risk
↓
Options / Futures
↓
Speculation & Liquidity
```

## Carry Trade Concept

```text
Borrow Low
↓
Convert Currency
↓
Invest High
↓
Earn Spread
```

Example:

* Borrow JPY at low rates
* Invest in USD assets at higher rates

Risk:

* FX movement
