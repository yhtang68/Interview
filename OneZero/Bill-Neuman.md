# 4. Questions for Bill Neuman

## 4.1 Product

What customer problems are currently the hardest for oneZero to solve?

## 4.2 Quality

What does product quality mean at oneZero beyond defect counts?

## 4.3 Customer Protection

How does oneZero think about helping customers avoid mistakes, detect them early, and recover safely when mistakes inevitably happen?

## 4.4 Quality Philosophy

When you think about product quality, how much emphasis is placed on prevention versus recovery?

## 4.5 Security & Trust

How does oneZero balance security, auditability, and usability across traders, brokers, and administrators?

## 4.6 Identity & Access

What are the biggest challenges around authentication, authorization, and operational security as the platform scales?

## 4.7 Reliability

How does oneZero think about resiliency and disaster recovery across trading, pricing, and data systems?

## 4.8 Growth

Which product area do you see driving the most growth over the next few years?

## 4.9 Profitability & Cost

Across oneZero's product areas or customer segments, which are the most profitable today, and which are the most costly to support or scale?
